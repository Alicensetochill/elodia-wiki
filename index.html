Setting

Elodia is an ancient land. The bones of many great civilisation litter the land, forgotten. Dusty tombs and labyrinthine halls twist and turn down into the depths of the earth sheltering terrible monsters and more terrible secrets. New kingdoms and people now build on the ruins of what was, ignorant to the fates of the many empires since past. This new order is a fragile and delicate thing. In lavish courts and in gloomy dark rooms, beasts of ambition seek to harness the ancient powers of kingdoms long past and upend the existing order. Can our brave adventurers survive this tumultuous land?

Realms

Kingdom of Carthania

The Kingdom of Carthania is a prosperous kingdom seated in the port city of Seagate. Although the crown held great power once upon a time, nowadays the power lies in the Grand Council, the assembly of representatives from the major merchant and noble families throughout the kingdom. Their position of power is built upon their strong navy and control of commerce.

Greenmarch Confederation

The Greenmarch Confederation is collection of settlements from Argywn to Fen Moor. Formerly an autonomous territory of the Kingdom of Karak-Morn, now independent. Since the fall of Karak-Morn, there has been great unrest throughout the federation, the Council of Argwyn constantly fights and bickers as the country is beset by mercenary warlords, raiders and monsters.

Lorinwood

Capital of the Enchanted Woods, the city encircles and protects *The Hallowed Oak*. They have a friendly relationship with the people of Argwyn, but are otherwise suspicious of outsiders. It is said the forest twists turns and changes to confuse and deter unwelcome visitors.

Karak-Morn

Once a proud dwarven city stronghold, its grand halls and corridoors twist deep down into the earth. 30 years ago the city was stormed from its deepest depths and occupied by *Zarash* and his drow army, who now rules with and iron fist. Many dwarven exiles can be found in nearby settlements, seeking refuge from *Zarash's* brutal occupation.

Karak-Kul

An ancient dwarven kingdom, ruled by old king *Zunthir*. The kingdom is know for its guilded halls, fabulous temples and glittering caves. Many pilgrims perish from giant attacks or the harsh weather, travelling overland to the city.

Gods

The main pantheon for the Gods of Elodia. These Gods names are the common names given to these deities by the people of Carthania & Greenmarch. Other cultures have different names for the Gods and have different interpretations and traditions associated with them. The gods listed do not comprise all the gods worshipped, and many other more minor gods are worshipped also, these are just the most prevalent gods in this region of the world.

Eloda, the Earth-Mother

The daughter of *Ayra* and *Kallias*, now dead. As the creator and mother of the mortal races she loved and protected them in the age before time. After her death and the destruction of the *Silver Gardens*, she formed herself into the new lands of the mortal realm, as a final shelter for her children.

Ayra

Domains: Arcana, Knowledge, Peace, Fate, Pegasi
Symbol: White Pegasus

Ayra, Queen of Celestia, is a God above Gods and maintains peace in the heavenly realms. As the goddess of magic and fate, she preserves the balance of the Pantheon and is said to seldom intervene in the lives of mortals, except to thwart the meddling of other gods. After the death of Eloda, Ayra took responsibility for tending her grave. It is said that Ayra bestowed magic upon the mortal races so they could protect Eloda without the protection of the Earth-Mother.

Kallias

Domains: War, Order, Light, Dragons
Symbol: Golden Dragon

Kallias, King of Celestia, rules alongside Ayra in enforcing order throughout the Celestial Pantheon. As the Divine Protector of Celestia, he is said to watch over Elodia's battlefields in search of the worthiest warriors to join his army in the Celestial War. He cares little for mortal affairs beyond testing his subjects through hardship and conflict to prove their worth. His ferocity in battle is legendary, and his wrath, if invoked, is terrible.

Erebion

Domains: War, Trickery
Symbol: Hooked Finger

Erebion is a powerful primordial god, equal in stature to Ayra and Kallias. Ancient tales recount his betrayal during the Unforgotten War, an act that led to the death of Eloda and the destruction of the Silver Garden by the Chaos Beast Lygoron. Kallias' fury over this betrayal sparked the conflict now known as the Celestial War. Earthquakes, storms, and floods are often attributed to the continuing clashes between these divine powers.

Leshia

Domains: Knowledge, Water
Symbol: Conical Seashell

Leshia is the goddess of Knowledge and History. For her faithful, the pursuit and preservation of knowledge are sacred duties. Scholars and acolytes often travel to the shores of Elodia, hoping to receive forgotten wisdom whispered through washed-up seashells blessed by Leshia.

Oseph

Domains: Death, Grave, Afterlife
Symbol: Raven's Head

Oseph is the god of Death and the Afterlife. It is said that they appear to dying souls in the form of a robed kenku and guide them along the River of Loss to the Pale Hall, where departed spirits may find eternal rest.

Mirhis / Miu-ai

Domains: Trickery, Twilight
Symbol: Black Cat

Mirhis, also known as Miu-ai, is the god of Tricks, Shadows, and the Poor. Worshipped by thieves, beggars, and the downtrodden, they are often depicted stealing from the greedy and wealthy before redistributing those treasures to the penniless.

Luthian

Domains: Nature, Twilight, Trickery, Lycanthropy, Hunting
Symbol: Four-Eyed Wolf

Luthian is the god of the Hunt and the Wild. Revered by hunters and feared by farmers, they are said to roam the deep forests in the form of a massive black dire wolf with four glowing red eyes. Callous and unpredictable, Luthian's favor is fickle, and hunters often pray for their blessing lest fortune turn against them.

Benethar

Domains: Forge, Light
Symbol: Constellation of Stars in a Loop

Benethar is the god of the Stars and the Forge. The stars themselves are said to be his creation. While blacksmiths are among his most devoted followers, artists, poets, and dancers also seek his favor, for Benethar is believed to bless all who bring beauty into the world.

Hroska

Domains: Life, Nature, Order, Shepherds, Dogs
Symbol: Shepherd's Crook

Hroska is the god of Birth, Shepherds, Life, and Fertility. Shrines dedicated to him are commonly found in hospitals and shepherds' homes. Dogs, believed to be a gift from Hroska to mortalkind, are often sheltered in his temples and shrines, as they are thought to possess divine abilities to ease suffering and sense illness.

Tomyris

Domains: Ambition, Competition, Glory, Horses, War
Symbol: Iron Horseshoe

Tomyris is the goddess of Glory, Ambition, Horses, and War. Free-spirited warriors and those seeking personal renown are drawn to her as a symbol of strength and martial excellence. Her worship is common among raiders, gladiators, duelists, and all who seek victory through their own prowess.

Aeldris

Domains: Tempest, Arcana
Symbol: Purple Roc

Aeldris is the god of Wind and Storms, commonly worshipped by sailors, nomads, and those who dwell far from civilization. Organized temples are rare, with remote shrines along mountain trails and windswept steppes being far more common. Followers often wear feather charms around their necks, believing them to strengthen their connection to the Weave.

Cirawen

Domains: Sleep, Dreams, Peace, Twilight
Symbol: Closed Eye

Cirawen is the goddess of Dusk, Dreams, and Prophecy. Her acolytes frequently serve as soothsayers and spiritual guides. Many revere the inhalation of Dreammist, a sacred substance believed to grant visions of possible futures.
